Frequently asked questions

The questions wholesale, retail and uniform buyers ask us most — answered directly.

Sourcing basics

MOQ, sampling and lead times

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ) for custom knitwear?

Our standard MOQ is 500 pieces per style. Mixed colours and mixed sizes are accepted within one style; a single colour carries a minimum of 200 pieces. Fully customised yarns or custom-dyed colours require 800–1,000 pieces per style per colour. Contact us with your quantity and we will confirm feasibility within one working day.

How long does sampling take?

In the off-season, 3–7 days from approved tech pack to first sample. In peak season (roughly June–September), 7–12 days. Sampling cost depends on the style; it is typically credited against your bulk order.

What is your bulk production lead time?

Standard programmes: 45–60 days after order confirmation. Styles with complex workmanship: 60–75 days. In peak season add 10–15 days. We confirm the exact schedule per style before you commit — and we never promise a date we cannot protect.

What gauges can you knit?

The full commercial range: 3G to 16G — from heavy 3G/5G chunky cables and outerwear knits to fine 12G/16G cotton and silk-blend tops. Computerised flat knitting across our partner factories covers all of these.

Products & materials

What we can make

What product categories do you supply?

Men's, women's and kids' knitwear: pullovers, cardigans, hoodies, knit dresses, vests and matching sets — for wholesale, private label, retail programmes and corporate/uniform orders. Full-category knit coverage: if it is made on a flat knitting machine, we can produce it.

Which yarn compositions are available?

All commercial ranges: acrylic, cotton and viscose, wool blends, wool, cashmere, and speciality fibres such as mohair and alpaca. GRS-certified recycled fibres and custom blend ratios are available on request. Exact composition and price are confirmed per style.

Can you work from a photo or sketch, not just a tech pack?

Yes. Send a photo, a sketch or a reference link — our development team builds a feasibility answer and a costing within one working day, then a sample follows the normal sampling schedule.

Do you supply small boutique buyers or only large programmes?

Both. Our monthly capacity of 50,000–100,000 pieces comes from 3–5 partner factories, so we can run a 500-piece boutique programme and a 20,000-piece chain-store programme in parallel — one contact for both.

Terms, quality & trust

How we work

What are your payment terms?

Standard terms: T/T 30% deposit, 70% against copy of Bill of Lading. Fully customised programmes (custom yarn or colours) carry a 40–50% deposit. LC terms can be discussed for established buyers.

Do you have your own factory? Can we audit it?

We consolidate 3–5 long-term partner factories in China's largest knitwear cluster (Zhejiang). We do not claim to be a mill — that is exactly why you get a wider style range and peak-season flexibility. Factory audits and third-party inspections are welcome and can be arranged; partner factory visits in Zhejiang can be booked by appointment.

Can you arrange third-party inspection (SGS, BV, Intertek)?

Yes. We do not present our own certificates as a seller — instead, we arrange third-party inspections by SGS, Bureau Veritas or Intertek at your request, and support customer-specified testing before shipment.

What are your indicative FOB prices?

Indicative FOB ranges (for budgeting only, final price per style): acrylic US$2.5–4.5, cotton/viscose US$3.5–6.5, wool blends US$5.5–9.5, wool US$8.5–15, cashmere US$18–45 per piece. Send your style and quantity for an exact quotation within one working day.

Which markets and shipping routes do you cover?

Main exports: Russia and CIS (Yiwu–Moscow direct rail, about 15 days transit), the Middle East (Jebel Ali direct, 18–22 days), Southeast Asia (7–12 days) and Central Asia (rail via Alashankou/Khorgos). FOB, CIF and door-to-door terms are available; port of loading is Ningbo or Shanghai.
