The Zhejiang logistics edge: order to Moscow in about 75 days

The Yiwu–Moscow direct rail service runs roughly three departures a week and reaches Moscow in 15 days, versus 40+ days by traditional sea routing via the Russian Far East. For a seasonal programme that difference is often the difference between catching the season and missing it.
