Production base

Why our production base matters

Our partner factories sit inside China's largest knitwear cluster. This region accounts for roughly 7 of every 10 sweaters produced worldwide, and holds the entire chain — yarn, knitting, linking, washing, finishing — within a compact radius. For a buyer that means yarn is on hand, sampling is measured in days rather than weeks, and capacity can be re-allocated when a programme needs it.
